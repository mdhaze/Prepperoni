## CDC bleach, 5% to 9% sodium hypochlorite, unscented

If cloudy, murky, colored, or very cold: double the amount, after filtering or settling.

| volume | clear water |
| --- | --- |
| 1 quart or 1 liter | 2 drops, or 0.1 mL |
| 1 gallon | 8 drops, or 0.5 mL, or a little less than 1/8 teaspoon |
| 5 gallons | 40 drops, or 2.5 mL, or 1/2 teaspoon |

Wait 30 minutes. Slight chlorine smell expected. No scent additives. Never mix with ammonia. Label instructions win if they exist.

1% sodium hypochlorite is a different table (5x the 5–9% volumes). If concentration is unknown, refuse.

## CDC potassium iodide, single dose, 24-hour coverage

Only for radioactive iodine. Useless against external gamma and other isotopes. Officials direct use. Repeat no sooner than 24 hours, and only if officials say exposure continues.

| group | dose | 130 mg tablets | 65 mg tablets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| birth through 1 month | 16 mg | use oral solution | 1/4 |
| 1 month through 3 years | 32 mg | use oral solution | 1/2 |
| over 3 through 12 years | 65 mg | 1/2 | 1 |
| 12 through 18, under 150 lb | 65 mg | 1/2 | 1 |
| 12 through 18, over 150 lb | 130 mg | 1 | 2 |
| adults over 18 through 40 | 130 mg | 1 | 2 |
| pregnant or lactating | 130 mg | 1 | 2 |
| adults over 40 | 130 mg only if predicted thyroid exposure is high (CDC threshold ≥ 500) | 1 | 2 |

Infants: more than one dose can harm later development. Oral solution 65 mg/mL: 0.25 mL, 0.5 mL, 1 mL, 2 mL matching the rows above.

## Fallout approximation, not a measurement

For a nuclear detonation's early fallout, a common civil-defense approximation is that dose rate drops by a factor of about 10 when time since burst increases by a factor of about 7 (1 h to 7 h to 49 h to 2 weeks). State that it is an approximation. Do not use it for a power-plant release unless the user has a measured dose rate, in which case use the measurement and the stay-time tool instead.
